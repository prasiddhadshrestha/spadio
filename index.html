About This Sound

How It Works: Earth Jukebox uses parameter-mapping sonification—image brightness, color hue, and vertical position control pitch, volume, and rhythm. While parameter-mapping is a standard technique, the specific mapping rules, style names (Sparkle, Flow, Layers, Field, Duet), and thresholds here are original team design choices, not an established standard and not yet validated in formal listening studies.

Credits: Imagery and visualizations from NASA Scientific Visualization Studio (SVS) & Earth Information Center. Concept inspired by NASA's "Data Sonification: Caldwell 73" (SVS 13786).

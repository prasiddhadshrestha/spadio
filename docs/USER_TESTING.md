# Blind & Low-Vision User Testing Guide

Thank you for testing **Earth Jukebox**. Because our sound mappings are original design choices rather than established standards, your feedback directly shapes how we tune pitch, contrast, and captions.

## Session Protocol (15 Minutes)
1. **Track Exploration (Keyboard Only):** Navigate the Track Shelf using arrow keys and press Enter on *Aurora & City Lights* and *Night Lights*.
2. **Listen & Compare:** Press **Play** and listen to one full 48-band sweep.
3. **Test Captions & Narration:** Toggle **Narration** on or listen with your screen reader's `aria-live` announcements.
4. **Test Audio Gallery:** Scroll to *Listen Without the App* and play a pre-rendered WAV track.

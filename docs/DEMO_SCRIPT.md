# 3-Minute Demo Script

1. **Problem (20 s):** NASA's Earth Information Center visualizations are breathtaking—but purely visual, excluding blind and low-vision audiences.
2. **Aurora Globe, Duet (40 s):** Select *Aurora & City Lights*. Point out the progressive *"What the computer sees"* 48-band strip building live as the scan moves: cyan ocean currents become glassy sine pads, dark space is silence, orange city lights become bell-like plucks, panning left to right.
3. **Night Lights 2012–2023, Sparkle (40 s):** Switch to *Night Lights (SVS 5313)*. Notice how sparse urban dots trigger crisp, isolated bell taps against silent oceans.
4. **GEOS Aerosols, Layers (30 s):** Switch to *GEOS Aerosols (SVS 5669)*. Three color families—dust, smoke, sea salt—play as three distinct instruments at once.
5. **Accessibility & Upload (30 s):** Toggle **High Contrast**, show **Live Captions** (`aria-live="polite"`), upload any image with **Use another frame**, and scroll to the **Listen without the app** pre-rendered WAV gallery.
6. **Close (20 s):** *"Everything you heard is also described in text. The planet is changing, and now you can hear it."*
